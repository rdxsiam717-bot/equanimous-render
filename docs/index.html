Make voiceovers in your own voice

This site is the control panel for the Equanimous narrator voice. Upload a short recording of your voice, and it becomes a voice clone. Then paste any script, pick your clone, and generate a voiceover on GitHub's free runners — and download the MP3 straight from your browser.

No servers, no subscriptions, no card. The site is a static page (GitHub Pages). It talks to GitHub's API with a token you paste once and that stays only in your browser.

How it works

  1. Upload a voice. On My Voices, upload a 5–10 second recording of clear speech — ideally your own voice reading two motivational lines with the energy you want. It is saved in the repo under references/ and becomes a clone.
  2. Pick a clone and paste a script. On Generate, choose the clone, the provider and the speed, then paste the script.
  3. Generate and download. The voiceover is rendered on GitHub's free runners (a 60-second voiceover takes a few minutes). When it finishes, download the MP3 from this page.

Providers

Chatterbox Nano — main

Open-source (MIT licence), runs on CPU, and clones your reference voice so the narration sounds like you — warm, expressive and human. This is the default.

Kokoro — fallback

Open-source (Apache 2.0), very fast on CPU. If Chatterbox fails for any reason, the workflow falls back to Kokoro's male voice bm_daniel. Both licences are commercial-safe for a monetised channel.

The provider actually used for each voiceover is recorded in the summary that comes back with the MP3, so you always know which one spoke.
